---
name: turn-recurrence-into-a-playbook
description: Convert an evidence-backed repeated incident pattern into a concise, revisable investigation and verification playbook.
origin: professional-practice
kind: practice
times_applied: 0
---

When independent incident evidence supports a repeated operational pattern, extract the reusable
part: what to notice, how to investigate it, what competing explanation to disconfirm, and how
to verify recovery later. Do not promote a single memorable incident into a playbook just because
it is vivid.

- Keep provenance in frontmatter, but use placeholders in the playbook body so it remains useful
  beyond one incident, service instance, timestamp, or trace.
- A useful playbook is a decision aid, not a hidden alert rule: reason from current evidence and
  do not encode numeric firing thresholds or fixed severity mappings.
- Revisit a playbook when later evidence contradicts it or adds a better discriminator. Revise
  it visibly and retain the review record rather than silently overwriting the old assumption.
- Prefer one focused playbook that clarifies a distinct repeated pattern over several overlapping
  files that future responders cannot choose between.
